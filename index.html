Lech’s conjecture in equal characteristic

Srikanth B. Iyengar, Linquan Ma, and Mark Walker

Our exposition of the equal-characteristic argument. We believe the proofs are correct, but this draft is much rougher than we would ordinarily circulate. Our current understanding and confidence are closer to those of referees assessing a proof than of authors who have fully developed and internalized every aspect of it. The revised proofs in Section 3 were also obtained using ChatGPT Astra.

We welcome corrections, suggestions, and pointers to earlier work that may deserve credit.

I am part of the commutative algebra and algebraic geometry group in my department.
